POE2HTC — Path of Exile 2 crafting calculator

Tell it the base and the modifiers you want, and it works out how to craft the item and what that will cost. The probabilities are computed exactly from Path of Exile 2's real modifier weight pools — not simulated — and every route is priced from live poe.ninja market data.

  • Plan a craft from a white base, or from an item you are already holding
  • The cheapest route and the surest route, with the odds for each
  • True expected cost — what the craft costs playing optimally, recovering after a bad roll rather than starting over
  • Budget mode, currency you do not own, and shareable links

Free, no account, nothing to install — the whole engine runs in your browser.
